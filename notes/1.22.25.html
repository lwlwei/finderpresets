Fixed

  • Pressing on the side bar's title ("Side Bar") now drags the whole bar, like pressing on the empty part of its header. Before, in English and other languages with a longer title, the title text swallowed the press and nothing moved.

Syncing

  • See how syncing is doing: Settings ▸ General ▸ Data & Backup now shows this Mac's name, which Mac saved to the backup folder last and when, whether another Mac's changes are waiting (with a Review… button), and how many files iCloud is still bringing down. (A Mac names itself in the backup once it runs this version.)

Shortcuts and Siri

  • Open Command Palette and Create Folders from Template are new actions. The second creates one of your folder templates in a folder you give, or else in the folder in front in Finder (the Mac App Store edition opens Create Preset Folders with the template ready).

Tips & Tricks

  • New tips: the presets you use most come first, how to see how syncing is doing, and where to read what's new before and after updating. The Shortcuts tip lists the new actions.

Command palette

  • Tips now come after commands, presets and folders when you search, instead of ahead of them.

Under the hood

  • The release process also checks, with a real mouse, dragging a folder out of the side bar's pop-up page and dropping one on the menu bar icon, and a script takes pictures of every window in each language and in dark mode for a look before a release.
  • Development builds keep their own data folder, so running one never touches the installed app's presets and side bar.
