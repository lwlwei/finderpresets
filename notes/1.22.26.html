Side bar

  • The side bar no longer covers FinderPresets' own windows. It still stays above other apps' Open and Save panels, but steps behind Tips, Settings, Quick Jump and the other FinderPresets windows while you use them.
  • When the bar is resized shorter than its welcome content, the header stays visible and the suggestions scroll, instead of overflowing at both ends.

Tips & Tricks

  • Redrawn animations for the tips on dragging several Quick Jump results at once, the presets you use most, seeing how syncing is doing, and where to read what's new. The steps of the first two tips are worded more clearly.

Speed

  • Lighter on the Mac. The looping animations (the empty side bar's, and the ones in Tips & Tricks) draw fewer frames a second and stop while their window can't be seen, so an empty side bar left on screen no longer keeps the processor and the display busy.
