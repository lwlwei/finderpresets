App groups

  • Start a group of apps with one shortcut. Menu bar icon ▸ App Groups… lets you list apps, record a shortcut and start them together, in order. Apps that are already running come to the front, and an app that is no longer installed is reported while the rest still start.
  • Works with Stage Manager. Set a group to “Stage Manager: bring the first app to the front”: put the apps in one stage by hand once, and the shortcut then brings the whole stage back. Keep the first app open: if you quit it, only it comes back. There is a tip with the details.

Side bar

  • A list of the folders open in Finder (direct download edition). Show it like Recent, or as one row that looks like a folder of the bar and opens a list of the bar's own rows beside it, from which you can open, drag out or pin a folder.
  • Recent and Open in Finder can each be shown, collapsed to their heading, or hidden. Click a heading to open or collapse it.
  • Set them in Settings ▸ General ▸ Side Bar, or from the side bar's “＋” menu.

Folder cloner

  • Pick the source or destination from the folders open in Finder with the new Open in Finder menu on each row (direct download edition).
  • The whole path is shown, wrapped over as many lines as it needs, instead of being cut short in the middle.

Under the hood

  • The release tests wait for a slow or busy Mac instead of failing, and a test that made 300 000 files now makes a thousand.
