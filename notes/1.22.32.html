Menu bar

  • The menu bar icon's menu is shorter. On a new install it shows your presets, Manage Presets, Quick Jump, the command palette and the side bar; everything else is under More. Hold Option while clicking the icon to see every command at once.
  • Choose what stays on top in Settings ▸ General ▸ Menu Bar Menu, or switch between “Short menu” and “Everything on top” with one button. If you already use the app, your menu stays as it is until you change it.
  • A tip, “Keep the menu bar menu short”, explains it.

Speed

  • Opening the menu bar menu checks the setup state once and skips the slowest query.
