Side bar

  • Categories can carry a color tag, like Finder's: right-click a category ▸ Tag Color. The color shows on the category, and goes with Export and the backup.
  • Recent and Open in Finder can be moved up and down: drag the ≡ that shows when the mouse is on the list, or use Move Up / Move Down in its menu. Folders in a second-level list can be moved up and down too, with their own ≡, and stay in their category.
  • The shelf keeps files where they are, and they leave as real files. Dragging one out into a Finder window used to make an alias; now it makes a real copy. The Option copy is gone. A line now sets the lists the app makes (shelf, Recent, Open in Finder) apart from the folders you pinned.
  • Hold Option while dropping on the bar to pin the folder that holds the file, or a folder's parent folder. The two layouts of those lists are now called List and Second-level menu.

Menu bar and shortcuts

  • The menu bar icon's menu is short for everyone: presets, Manage Presets, Quick Jump, the command palette and the side bar; the rest is under More (Option-click shows everything). The side bar at the mouse is no longer a menu command, but keeps its shortcut and settings.
  • Every command can have a global shortcut: Settings ▸ Shortcuts ▸ Other commands adds Manage Presets, App Groups, Tips, What's New, Help, Undo, Close the last preset's windows and Check for Updates. The shortcut summary lists them and the app groups.

Backup

  • App groups are now in the backup folder (without their shortcuts), and the settings sync also carries the menu bar menu, the Dock icon and the side bar's list options. A restore adds only what is missing.

Smaller things

  • “Create the folder itself” in Folder Cloner is off by default; opening the menu bar menu is a little quicker.
