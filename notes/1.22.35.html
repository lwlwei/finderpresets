Tips & Tricks

  • Every demonstration is redrawn so you can see how to do it. Each one now shows the real window with real labels, the mouse pointer and its clicks, and what changes. The old ones were pictures with no words, such as the one for the menu bar menu, which only showed some keys.
  • New tips for second-level lists, holding ⌥ while dropping on the side bar, folders open in Finder, hiding the Dock icon, color tags, reordering the lists, a shortcut for every command, and syncing deletions between Macs.

Side bar

  • Folders can carry color tags too, not only categories: right-click ▸ Tag Color. + ▸ Show Only a Color shows just one color, and ✕ on the bar above brings everything back.

Backup

  • What you delete on one Mac is offered for deletion on your other Macs. A message lists the names and you choose Delete Them Here Too, Keep Them or Not Now. What you deleted on purpose is never brought back by another Mac's copy.

Settings

  • The long lists (the menu bar commands, and the shortcuts for the other commands) start tucked away and open while you search.
