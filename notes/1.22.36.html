Side bar

  • Typing letters to find a folder works again. Click the Side Bar's title once to give it the keyboard (clicking a folder does not, so a click there can still send an Open or Save window to it), then type part of a name or alias, or use ↑↓, and press Return.
  • It also finds the folders inside a category shown as a second-level menu: that list opens with the match picked, and typing goes on in the list.
