Side bar

  • Rest the mouse on the side bar and it takes the keyboard: type part of a folder's name or alias to find it, move with ↑↓, press Return to open it. No click is needed, and the keyboard goes back to the other app when the mouse leaves.
  • It never takes the keyboard while an Open or Save window is in front (a click on a folder must still send that window there), while you are typing in a text field of another app, while you are dragging, or when the mouse only passes by. It can be switched off in Settings ▸ Side Bar.
  • Clicking the title of the bar still gives it the keyboard on purpose.

Fixes

  • The notice shown after an unexpected quit is now read out by VoiceOver.
  • Development builds put the preferences of the installed app back when they quit (they had been leaving a pinned edge and height behind).
