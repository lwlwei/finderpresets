Settings

  • Settings has a window of its own. Menu bar icon ▸ Settings… (⌘,), or the app menu's Settings…; it can stay open next to the preset list and be resized. Manage Presets… is now ⇧⌘, in the app menu. The Tips point to it.
  • New side bar options: how long the bar waits before hiding, how long the mouse must rest before it takes the keyboard, how many recent folders and folders open in Finder it lists. New: how long short notices stay on screen. All of them follow you between Macs with “Also sync settings”.
