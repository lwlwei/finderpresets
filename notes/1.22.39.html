Settings, redesigned

  • Seven pages down the left, with a title and a line of what each is for: General, Opening Presets, Menu Bar, Shortcuts, Side Bar, Data & Backup, and Permissions & Checks. The settings used to be one long page split into four tabs, with start-up, appearance and checks mixed together. Search stays at the top and looks through every page. A dot on Permissions & Checks says something needs attention.
  • The window is called Settings. The gear icon is on Settings… now (menu bar icon ▸ Settings…, ⌘,), and the preset list is Manage Presets… (⇧⌘,).

Reopen what you closed

  • “Reopen the windows that were just closed”: after FinderPresets closes a preset's windows, or every Finder window, one command in the menu bar icon's menu opens those folders again, each once. It can have a shortcut in Settings ▸ Shortcuts ▸ Other commands. (Direct download edition; only windows closed by FinderPresets are remembered.)
