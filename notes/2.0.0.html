Settings

  • Settings is now one clear window. Seven pages with a search box — General, Opening Presets, Menu Bar, Shortcuts, Side Bar, Data & Backup, Permissions & Checks — plus a new About page. The gear icon now opens it.
  • About holds the rest. Version, the developer credit and (in the Mac App Store edition) your purchase, activation and Lifetime badge moved here from the preset list.
  • Update history. About lists every version, newest first; click one to read everything it brought. What's New ends with "More improvements and refinements" and an All Updates… button.

Shortcuts

  • Easier to scan. Manage Presets is now Window Presets, the first row; App Groups sits under Quick Jump; Close All Windows and Command Palette moved into Other Commands; "shortcut" is dropped from names that already say it.

Menu Bar

  • Settings… sits right above Quit, with ⌘, as usual.
