Easier to use

  • A folder with square brackets in its name opens as it is. Pick or drop “[Client] Project” (or “[date] Reports”, “[x] misc”) and it is a folder, not a question about a “Client”; a preset that already holds one offers “Open as a folder” under the path.
  • The command palette closes, reopens and undoes too: type “close” for Close all Finder windows, Close the windows of the last preset and Reopen the windows that were just closed; “undo” finds Undo the last folder creation. Add to Preset now says what it added (and works with no preset yet), and a link to a preset that does not exist says so.
  • A clearer editor and settings: “Open Now” and “Preview What Will Open…” explain themselves in a tooltip, the view picker says “Leave as is” instead of “None”, the Manage Presets window opens wider and remembers its size, and the Side Bar page is grouped under small headings.
  • The presets list does more: search understands accents and pinyin (“kehu”, “kh”), a folder dropped twice is listed once, and a preset can be deleted from the right-click menu or with the Delete key; ⌘Z brings it back.
  • Chinese reads better: the preset is 预设 everywhere (menus and the editor said 预置), and Traditional Chinese uses 裡 instead of 里 in 96 texts and Taiwan's words (啟動, 執行, 伺服器, 自訂, 選取).

Steadier

  • A window position saved on a monitor that is no longer connected no longer puts Finder windows out of reach: they come back on the main display.
  • A presets file edited by hand cannot stop the app: two presets with one id, or a window frame holding NaN or a huge number, are repaired or ignored instead of crashing the command palette or opening a preset.
  • Quieter in the background: the Open/Save panel watcher rests while nobody is at the Mac, and choosing a preset no longer rewrites the presets file (and its backup copy).
