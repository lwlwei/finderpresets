Easier to use

  • Select several presets in Manage Presets (⌘-click or Shift-click) and Export, Pin or Delete them together; one Undo brings a deletion back. Right-click a preset for “Export…”.
  • “Share Presets…” in Settings ▸ Data & Backup: tick the presets to send to another Mac. “Select All” and “Select None” work in this list and in the import list.
  • “Import Presets…” shows a preview first: how many folders are missing, what to do with a preset of the same name (keep both, skip or replace), and a path prefix you can replace, such as another user name.
  • “Locate…” when a folder has moved: choose where it is now and the preset is updated.
  • Search ignores accents and understands pinyin. Traditional Chinese Siri wording is more natural.

Steadier

  • A shortcut that can't run now says why instead of failing silently.
  • Unreadable saved records are noticed: you get a notice and the original data is kept aside, instead of the list quietly losing entries.
