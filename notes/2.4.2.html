New (direct download version)

  • “Extra features” in Settings ▸ General. Nine optional features, each with its own switch and a line saying what it does. All are off until you switch them on, and the choice is part of the synced settings.
  • More for proxies and originals. “Remember what is in the original folders” makes the second search by name immediate, “Ask which one when several originals have the name” shows a list with folder, size and date, “Also go from an original to its proxy” works the other way round, and “Compare a proxy folder with its originals” adds a “Compare…” button that lists what is missing on either side.
  • Drop files straight into a folder. With “Move files dropped on a side bar folder into it, sorted by rule” on, files dropped on a folder in the side bar are moved into it (copied from another disk), into a subfolder named by your rules, such as the date or the extension; “Put Back” undoes it. “Mark the likely folders while dragging files” marks the folders that took such files before.
  • A side bar that knows the app in front. “Show a different tag of the side bar for each app” shows the folders with the tag you chose for that app, and “Remember the side of the docked side bar for each app” adds “Dock on the Other Side” to the docked bar's menu.
  • Archive a preset's folders. With “Archive a preset's folders and bring them back” on, a preset's right-click menu gets “Archive Folders…”, which moves its folders to a place you choose, and “Restore Archived Folders”, which puts them back.
  • The side bar comes to the Finder window you drag from. Tick “Dock it beside a Finder window while dragging files out of it” and the bar appears right beside that window, then goes a moment after you let go. Off by default; needs no extra permission.

Better

  • The pop-up beside Open and Save windows can skip apps: add them under “Not for these apps”. A docked side bar now uses the display that shows most of the window, and a search by name lists first the originals whose folders are called like the proxy's.
  • Updates in Settings ▸ General (direct download version): “Check for updates automatically”, “Check Now”, and when the last check was.
