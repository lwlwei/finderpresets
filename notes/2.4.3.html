New

  • The pin keeps the side bar out. Click the 📌 pin at the top of the side bar and the bar no longer tucks away; click it again and it tucks away when the mouse is elsewhere. Locking the layout has moved to the 🔒 lock beside it.

New in “Extra features” (direct download version)

  • Presets that open by themselves. “Open presets by themselves, at a time or with an app” opens a preset at a time of day (every day, or “Mon–Fri only”) or when an app is opened. A time missed by more than 10 minutes is skipped.
  • New-item badges. “Show how many new items a side bar folder got” shows “+3” on a folder that got three items since you last opened it from the side bar.
  • More control over dropped files. With the drop rules, “Try a name:” shows where a file would go, “When a file of that name is already there:” lets you keep both, replace, skip or be asked each time, and “Moved lately” lists the last drops, each with “Put Back”.
  • Safer archiving. “Archive Folders…” now shows each folder's size and the free room at the destination, refuses when there is not enough room, and puts everything back if one folder cannot be moved.
  • The comparison window does more. Every row can be shown in Finder, and “Export CSV…” saves the whole comparison as a table.

Better

  • “Remember what is in the original folders” lasts across restarts. The list of file names is kept on this Mac and used again only when nothing in the folder changed; switching the feature off deletes it.
